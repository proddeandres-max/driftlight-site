Driftlight

A fast, glowing arcade game you play with one finger.

Drag anywhere to drift. Catch sparks, chain combos, and double-tap to release a pulse that clears everything around you. Survive waves of shards, seekers, novas and splitters, and outlast six very different bosses.

Four modes: Time Waves, Spark Hunt, Shaped Waves and Chaos. Upgrades before each boss. Practice for any wave or boss.

No account, no ads, no in-app purchases, no internet needed.
