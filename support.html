Support

How do I play?

Drag anywhere to move your light. It follows your finger's movement, not its position. Catch five sparks to charge your pulse, then double-tap anywhere to release it. Tap How to play on the menu for the full guide.

How do I beat a boss?

Bosses can only be hurt by your pulse, and only at the right moment. Watch the hint line at the top of the screen. A white ring after a pulse means "not now".

How do I practise?

Long-press the menu to start from any wave or fight a single boss. Practice runs don't count for your records.

I can't hear any sound.

Driftlight respects the silent switch on the side of your iPhone. Check that it's off and that the in-game sound isn't muted.

Can I get the first-time tips back?

Yes: open How to play and reset the tips at the bottom of the page.

Will I lose my scores if I delete the app?

Yes. Scores are kept only on your phone, so deleting the app removes them.
