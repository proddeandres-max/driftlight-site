Driftlight Support Privacy

Privacy Policy

Last updated: 13 November 2026

Driftlight is made by Darkhole Apps. This policy explains what happens to your information when you use the app.

The short version

Driftlight does not collect, store or share any personal data. It works fully offline.

What the app stores

Your best scores, best waves, sound setting and which tips you have already seen are saved in a small file on your device. This file never leaves your device and is deleted when you delete the app.

What the app does not do

Driftlight has no account or sign-in, no analytics or tracking, no advertising, and no in-app purchases. It makes no network connections, does not use your location, and does not use the microphone or camera.

Children

Because no data is collected, Driftlight is suitable for all ages and does not knowingly collect information from children.

Changes

If this policy changes, the new version will be posted on this page with a new date.
